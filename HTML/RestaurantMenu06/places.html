Famous Places in India

1. Taj Mahal

A white marble mausoleum in Agra, built by Shah Jahan. One of the Seven Wonders of the World.

2. Jaipur City Palace

A royal palace in Jaipur that blends Rajput and Mughal architecture.

3. Goa Beaches

Famous for golden sand, water sports and vibrant nightlife.

4. Kerala Backwaters

A network of lagoons and canals, best explored by houseboat.

5. Hampi

The ruins of the Vijayanagara Empire, set among unusual boulder landscapes.
