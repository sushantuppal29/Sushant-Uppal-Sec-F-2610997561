POPULAR DESTINATIONS

1. Manali, Himachal Pradesh

Famous for beautiful mountains, snow-covered landscapes, adventure sports, and peaceful valleys.

2. Goa

Known for its beaches, water sports, Portuguese architecture, and vibrant holiday atmosphere.

3. Jaipur, Rajasthan

The Pink City is popular for its historic forts, royal palaces, traditional markets, and culture.

4. Shimla, Himachal Pradesh

A scenic hill station known for pleasant weather, mountain views, and the famous Mall Road.
